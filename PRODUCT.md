# Island Runner Product Vision

Island Runner helps people on Texada Island shop from retailers in Powell River and coordinate delivery home.

Customers use **Scout** to search for products, compare prices from retailers, choose the products they want, and build a multi-store shopping cart. Island Runner coordinates the purchases and delivery so customers can bring items from multiple stores together in one island delivery.

Customers should not need retailer accounts to use the Island Runner shopping experience. The product should make it straightforward to discover options, make their own choices, and review what they want before proceeding.

The experience should remain simple, accessible, and clear about product information, prices, purchase responsibilities, and delivery arrangements.

## Purchasing model

Customers select products and submit their cart to Island Runner. An operator reviews the request and confirms the amount with the customer. The customer pays Island Runner, which purchases the selected products from retailers on the customer's behalf and coordinates delivery to Texada Island. Payment collection is part of the intended model but is not yet implemented.

## Scout

Scout is a shopping assistant, not simply a collection of retailer search links. It should present a small number of relevant product options and, where the information is reliably available, show each option's retailer, size, price, and availability. Customers decide which options enter their cart. Prices or availability that have not been verified must never be presented as confirmed.

## UX principles

- **Progressive disclosure:** Present the information and choices customers need at each step, with more detail available when it helps.
- **Minimize cognitive load:** Keep choices and explanations clear and manageable so customers can compare options and understand their cart.
- **Customer autonomy:** Customers control product selection and cart contents. Store-consolidation suggestions are deferred to a future iteration and must remain optional.
