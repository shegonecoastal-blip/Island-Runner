Try the Demo

Simple pickup and delivery

Island Runner collects retailer orders in Powell River and delivers them to Texada. Customers always buy and pay retailers directly.

1. Shop directly

Choose and pay for your items with each retailer. Select store pickup when ordering.

2. Request a pickup

Share your retailer pickup confirmations together. Island Runner will review the arrangements and contact you before accepting the request.

3. We deliver

Collection in Powell River is Thursday, 12:30–4:30 p.m. Texada deliveries begin around 6:30 p.m.

Delivery fee

$20 for the first retailer pickup, plus $5 for each additional pickup.

This delivery fee is separate from the merchandise you purchase and pay for directly through retailers.

A request is not a confirmed booking. Island Runner must contact you to confirm the pickup and delivery arrangements.

Questions about the demo?

This page is for exploring a fictional example. It does not accept pickup requests or payments.

Does Scout show live retailer prices?

No. Scout's products and prices on this page are fictional demonstration examples. Inventory, current prices, and local store availability have not been checked.

Does Island Runner buy my products?

No. Customers select, purchase, and pay for merchandise directly with retailers. Island Runner collects prepared orders and delivers them to Texada.

When are orders collected and delivered?

The planned Powell River collection window is Thursday, 12:30–4:30 p.m. Texada deliveries begin around 6:30 p.m.

How is the delivery fee calculated?

The delivery fee is $20 for the first retailer pickup plus $5 for each additional pickup. It is separate from merchandise costs paid to retailers.

Does the demo submit a delivery request?

No. Searching, selecting products, and using the cart only changes the sample page in your browser. No booking or customer information is sent.

When is a delivery booking confirmed?

A request must be reviewed. Island Runner will contact the customer to confirm pickup and delivery arrangements before accepting it.
