Questions? Scout's got answers! 🦊
These answers describe the planned shopping service. This prototype uses demonstration prices and does not accept real orders or payments.


How does Island Runner work?
Choose products with Scout. An operator reviews your order and confirms your final quote. Once you approve it and pay by e-Transfer, Island Runner shops for you and delivers across Texada.


When do you shop?
We shop in Powell River every Thursday.


When is the order deadline?
Submit your order by Wednesday at 6 p.m. Pacific Time for the Thursday shop.


How and when do I pay?
First, review and approve your final quote. Then pay Island Runner by e-Transfer by Wednesday at 10 p.m. Pacific Time, before we shop.


When will my shopping arrive?
Thursday home delivery across Texada is targeted for around 6 p.m. Arrival time may vary.


Where do you deliver?
We deliver to homes across Texada Island.


What happens if something is unavailable?
We skip unavailable items and refund the amount for those items. We do not replace them with something else.


Do you offer substitutions?
No. We never substitute products.


What happens if prices change?
Scout's current prices are demonstrations, not live prices. For the planned service, prices may change before purchase. You approve a final quote first, and may choose to authorize an optional 5% product-price buffer. Any unused prepaid funds are refunded.


What happens if only part of my order is available?
Unavailable items are skipped and refunded. If at least one item is delivered, the full agreed delivery fee applies. If the entire order is unavailable, we refund the full amount, including delivery.


Are Scout's prices guaranteed?
No. Current Scout prices are fictional demonstration prices, not verified live retailer prices or guaranteed purchase prices.
